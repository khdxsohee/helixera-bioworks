Sec. 04 — Method

Design. Build. Test. Learn.

Four disciplines, one loop. Tap each stage to expand.

  1. Targets are shortlisted by our ML design engine, which proposes and ranks candidate constructs against a fitness model trained on five years of internal assay data. Every brief leaves this stage with 40+ viable designs.

    ALPHAFOLD DOCKING · 40+ DESIGNS/BRIEF

  2. Oligo pools and gene fragments are assembled robotically across 384 wells — Golden Gate, Gibson and one-pot DOMINO reactions. Every construct carries a unique barcode from day zero.

    96 OLIGOS/DAY · GOLDEN GATE · GIBSON

  3. Phenotypic screens tie every result back to a construct ID. Flow cytometry, HPLC and amplicon sequencing run in parallel, so a hit never goes unexplained and a failure never goes unrecorded.

    FLOW · HPLC · AMPLICON-SEQ

  4. All assay data flows back into the design engine. Hit rates compound cycle over cycle — our average on-target rate has climbed from 31% in 2021 to 74% this year, on harder targets.

    CYCLE: 21 DAYS · HIT RATE 74%
